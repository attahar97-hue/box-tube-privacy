Box Tube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Box Tube Android app ("Box Tube", "the app", "we") handles information. Box Tube is published by Haris & Noor ("the developer"). If you have questions, write to us at attahar97@gmail.com.

In short: Box Tube has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not sell your data. Your downloads, settings and Vault stay on your phone. Text you type into search or the creator tools is sent to the services listed below so they can give you a result.

1. What Box Tube does

Box Tube is a browser and download manager for free and legal media. It lets you search and download videos, images and audio from sources that allow it, such as Pixabay, NASA, Wikimedia Commons and Internet Archive, and files you own. It also includes Creator Tools: a video analyzer and generators for tags, titles, descriptions, thumbnail ideas and hashtags.

Box Tube does not download videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or TikTok. Those sites open inside the app for viewing only.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or device identifiers.
  • We do not run advertising or third-party analytics inside the app.
  • We do not operate a server that stores your searches, links or downloads.

3. Information that stays on your device

  • Downloads: Files you download are saved on your phone in the Movies/Box Tube folder, and a list of them (title, size, status, file path) is kept in a local database.
  • Settings and history: Your theme, download quality, Wi-Fi only choice, language and recent searches are stored locally.
  • Vault: If you set a PIN for the Vault, it is stored only on your device. We cannot see it or recover it.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Information sent to third-party services

To give you results, the app sends some of what you type or paste to the services below. This information goes directly from your phone to that service.

ServiceUsed forWhat is sent
YouTube Data API v3 (Google) Video Analyzer: showing title, channel, category, tags and statistics of a public YouTube video The video ID from the link you paste
Gemini API (Google) Tag, title, description, thumbnail idea and hashtag generators The keyword, topic, style and platform you type
Pixabay API Search and download of free videos Your search words
NASA Image and Video Library Search and download of public-domain media Your search words
Wikimedia Commons Search and download of openly licensed media Your search words
Internet Archive Search and download of audio and video Your search words
Vimeo API Checking whether a Vimeo video allows downloads The video link you paste

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address and basic technical details of the request. Their handling of that information is covered by their own policies:

  • Google (YouTube and Gemini): Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service: youtube.com/t/terms
  • Pixabay: Pixabay Privacy Policy
  • NASA: NASA Privacy Policy
  • Wikimedia: Wikimedia Privacy Policy
  • Internet Archive: Internet Archive Terms and Privacy
  • Vimeo: Vimeo Privacy Policy

5. YouTube API Services

Box Tube uses YouTube API Services to show public information about a video you choose to analyze. By using this feature, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of YouTube is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Box Tube does not store YouTube data on any server and does not access your YouTube account.

6. The in-app browser

The Browser and YouTube tabs open third-party websites inside the app. Those websites can set their own cookies and collect their own data under their own policies. Box Tube does not control them. Sites such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are for viewing only, and no download button is shown on them.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load search results, web pages and downloads.
  • Notifications: to show download progress.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only): to save downloaded files to your phone.

8. Your content and copyright

Download only content you own or that is free to use. You are responsible for following the license of anything you download. Where a source asks for credit, Box Tube shows it next to the file.

9. Children

Box Tube is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to delete, but you can contact us if you have a concern.

10. Security

Requests to the services above are sent over secure (HTTPS) connections. Files and the Vault PIN are stored on your device, so keep your phone locked and updated.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example when a new source is added. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
